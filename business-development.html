Marketing Round-trip

export to marketing each quarter · import engagement after each issue

1 · Quarterly export

Only contacts who can get the newsletter: people who opted in on the form, plus existing business relationships (synced from Leads and County Boards). “Not asked” and opted-out contacts are never included.

2 · Import newsletter engagement

After an issue goes out, upload the platform’s activity or contact report (CSV). Contacts are matched by email. Clicks and replies go to the Follow-up Queue, unsubscribes are marked opted out.

3 · Keep the list complete

Pull every lead contact and County Board contact that has an email into this list as an existing relationship. Safe to run again: emails already here are skipped.

4 · Share the form

Add a short tag so you can see where each contact came from (letters, numbers, dashes).
