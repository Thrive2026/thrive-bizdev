Klip Referral & Conversion Analysis

source-by-source, all-time — a leads pipeline built from actual billing history
Pulled from the full Klip billing export (all-time through Sep 1, 2026; 2026 is a partial year). A referral source counts as Converted once a client has at least one encounter with a charge > 0 — that's the same definition used in the Klip_Referral_Conversion_Analysis.xlsx workbook delivered 9/9/26, and every number below reconciles to it. Dormant means the source has real historical volume (10+ engaged clients) but sent zero new clients in 2025 or 2026 — the relationship may still have active clients being served, but the referral pipeline from it has gone quiet. This only covers people Klip has an encounter for, so it's a floor on true referral volume, not a full count.

Org names here come straight from Klip's own billing records and won't always match how an organization is named in Leads or Referral Volume — the "Tracked?" column below is a best-effort text match, not a guarantee, so double-check before assuming a source has no lead.
